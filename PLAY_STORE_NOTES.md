# Play Store listing notes

CodeDrop is **not a bookmaker**. Users copy booking codes into SportyBet / Bet9ja / etc. and stake there. Our in-app wallet is for tipster subscriptions and earnings only.

## Content rating (typical answers — confirm with Play Console)

- **Gambling:** Yes, the *subject* is sports betting. There is no in-app staking, no real-money wagering through Google Play Billing, and no simulated casino.
- **Age:** 18+. Onboarding requires an 18+ confirmation.
- **User-generated content:** Yes (picks, rooms). Moderation: word filter, reports (auto-hide at 5), mute/ban, admin queue.
- Do **not** use “sure odds”, “fixed”, or “guaranteed” in the store listing or screenshots.
